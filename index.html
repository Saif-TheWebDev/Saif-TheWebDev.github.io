03 How it works

Four steps. No mystery.

You always know what is happening, what it costs and what comes next. No agency fog.

Typical timelines — marketing site 2–4 weeks, larger builds 6–10 weeks.

  1. 01

    Discover

    A working session on the business, the customer and the thing that is not working. You leave with a written brief and a sitemap.

    Deliverable · Brief & sitemap

  2. 02

    Define

    Structure, content model and a fixed scope with a price and a date. Nothing gets built before you approve it.

    Deliverable · Scope & fixed quote

  3. 03

    Design & Build

    Design and code in the same loop on a staging link you can watch update, with review points after each section.

    Deliverable · Staging build

  4. 04

    Launch & Refine

    Deploy, test, hand over — then four weeks of tuning against real traffic and the first Search Console data.

    Deliverable · Handover & 30 days

05 A few things to know

Good questions. Straight answers.

Still curious about something? Send a note and I’ll reply within one working day.

Ask a question
Can you work on my existing WordPress site?

Yes. Redesigns, new sections, responsive fixes, cleanup, performance work and custom front-end pieces. I’m happy to inherit a site rather than insist on starting over.

Do you build outside WordPress?

Yes. I also build with React, Next.js and Node.js, including web apps and headless setups when WordPress isn’t the right fit.

Can you help with SEO?

Yes. Technical SEO, local SEO, schema and page structure are built into the project rather than bolted on afterwards — with AEO and GEO in mind so answer engines can read the site too.

How long does a project take?

Marketing sites usually take 2 to 4 weeks. Larger builds and apps can take 6 to 10 weeks. You’ll get a fixed written timeline before anything starts.

What does it cost?

Projects are quoted as a fixed price after the first call. For complex applications, the work can be scoped in phases so the cost stays predictable.

Who do you work with?

Local businesses, professionals, founders and small teams anywhere in the world. Time zones are not a problem.
